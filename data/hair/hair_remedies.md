# Natural Hair Care and Self-Care

## General Principle

Natural remedies should be approached carefully. A substance
being natural does not automatically make it safe or effective
for every person.

Cosmora should prioritize gentle haircare habits over
unverified DIY treatments.


---

## Coconut Oil

Coconut oil may help reduce protein loss from hair and can
improve the feel and manageability of some hair types.

### General Use

- Use a small amount if tolerated.
- Apply primarily to the hair lengths when appropriate.
- Avoid excessive application if it causes scalp buildup.
- Stop using it if irritation develops.

Coconut oil should not be presented as a guaranteed treatment
for hair loss or scalp disease.


---

## Gentle Scalp Massage

Gentle scalp massage can be relaxing and may help distribute
natural oils across the scalp.

### General Approach

- Use fingertips rather than fingernails.
- Avoid aggressive scratching.
- Massage gently for a short period.
- Stop if pain or irritation occurs.

Scalp massage should not be presented as a proven treatment
for medical causes of hair loss.


---

## Aloe Vera

Aloe vera is sometimes used as a soothing ingredient for
the scalp.

### Precautions

- Use a simple preparation if tolerated.
- Test a small area first.
- Stop if burning, itching, redness, or irritation develops.
- Avoid applying it to damaged or infected skin.


---

## Gentle Detangling

Gentle detangling can reduce mechanical hair breakage.

### Good Practices

- Detangle from the ends upward.
- Avoid pulling forcefully through knots.
- Work through tangled sections gradually.
- Handle wet hair carefully.


---

## Reduce Heat Exposure

Reducing unnecessary heat is one of the simplest ways to
reduce mechanical and thermal damage.

### Helpful Habits

- Allow hair to air dry when practical.
- Use lower temperatures when heat styling.
- Avoid repeatedly applying heat to the same area.
- Give hair breaks from frequent heat styling.


---

## Avoid Lemon Juice on the Scalp

Lemon juice should not be recommended as a routine scalp
treatment.

It may cause irritation and can increase sensitivity to
sunlight.


---

## Avoid Baking Soda on Hair

Baking soda can be harsh and may contribute to dryness or
irritation.

It should not be recommended as a routine shampoo replacement.


---

## Avoid Undiluted Essential Oils

Essential oils can cause irritation or allergic reactions
when applied directly to the skin.

Cosmora should not recommend applying undiluted essential oils
directly to the scalp.


---

## Avoid Onion Juice as a Guaranteed Treatment

Onion juice is sometimes promoted online as a treatment for
hair loss.

Evidence is limited and irritation may occur.

Cosmora should not present onion juice as a guaranteed remedy
for hair growth.


---

## Avoid Aggressive Scratching

Scratching can irritate the scalp and may worsen inflammation
or cause skin damage.

### Better Approach

- Keep fingernails short.
- Cleanse the scalp gently.
- Identify potential sources of irritation.
- Seek professional advice if persistent itching occurs.


---

## Avoid Tight Hairstyles

Repeated tight hairstyles can place tension on hair follicles.

### Better Approach

- Use looser hairstyles.
- Alternate hairstyles.
- Avoid prolonged tension.
- Stop if the hairstyle causes scalp pain or tenderness.


---

## When Natural Remedies Should Not Be Used

Seek professional medical advice instead of relying on home
remedies when there is:

- Sudden hair loss
- Patchy hair loss
- Severe scalp inflammation
- Persistent scalp pain
- Bleeding or sores
- Signs of infection
- Severe itching
- Rapidly worsening symptoms
- Persistent unexplained symptoms


---

## General Hair Self-Care

The safest approach generally focuses on:

- Gentle washing
- Gentle detangling
- Reduced heat exposure
- Reduced chemical damage
- Adequate nutrition
- Adequate hydration
- Adequate sleep
- Stress management
- Avoiding unnecessary DIY treatments
