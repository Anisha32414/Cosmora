# Hair Health, Diet and Hydration

## General Nutrition

Hair growth requires adequate energy and nutrients because
hair follicles are metabolically active tissues.

A balanced diet can include:

- Protein
- Whole grains
- Fruits
- Vegetables
- Legumes
- Nuts and seeds
- Healthy fats
- Adequate fluids


---

## Protein

Hair is primarily composed of a protein called keratin.

Adequate dietary protein supports normal body functions and
hair production.

### Food Sources

Depending on dietary preferences:

- Lentils
- Chickpeas
- Beans
- Soy products
- Eggs
- Milk
- Yogurt
- Fish
- Chicken
- Nuts and seeds

Avoid recommending extreme high-protein diets.


---

## Iron

Iron is an essential nutrient involved in oxygen transport.

Food sources include:

- Lentils
- Beans
- Chickpeas
- Leafy green vegetables
- Meat
- Eggs
- Fortified foods

Low iron status can be associated with hair loss in some
individuals.

People with suspected iron deficiency should seek appropriate
professional evaluation rather than taking iron supplements
without guidance.


---

## Zinc

Zinc is an essential mineral involved in many biological
processes.

Food sources include:

- Beans
- Lentils
- Nuts
- Seeds
- Dairy products
- Eggs
- Meat
- Seafood

Both inadequate and excessive zinc intake can cause problems.

Avoid recommending high-dose zinc supplements without
professional guidance.


---

## Vitamin D

Vitamin D is important for overall health, and abnormal vitamin
D status has been investigated in relation to some hair-loss
conditions.

Food sources include:

- Fortified foods
- Eggs
- Fatty fish

Sunlight contributes to vitamin D production, but excessive
sun exposure should not be recommended as a treatment.

Testing and supplementation should be discussed with a
healthcare professional when deficiency is suspected.


---

## Biotin

Biotin is a B vitamin required for normal metabolism.

True biotin deficiency is uncommon.

Taking large amounts of biotin without a known deficiency
does not guarantee improved hair growth.

High-dose biotin supplements can also interfere with certain
laboratory tests.


---

## Vitamin C

Vitamin C supports normal body functions and helps with
absorption of non-heme iron from plant foods.

Sources include:

- Citrus fruits
- Guava
- Bell peppers
- Tomatoes
- Broccoli
- Kiwi
- Strawberries


---

## Healthy Fats

Healthy fats are part of a balanced diet.

Sources include:

- Nuts
- Seeds
- Avocado
- Fish
- Olive oil
- Other unsaturated plant oils


---

## Omega-3 Fatty Acids

Omega-3 fatty acids are found in foods such as:

- Fatty fish
- Flaxseeds
- Chia seeds
- Walnuts

They are useful nutrients within a balanced diet.

Cosmora should not claim that omega-3 foods alone can cure
hair loss.


---

## Hydration

Adequate fluid intake supports normal body functions.

### General Habits

- Drink fluids regularly.
- Increase fluid intake when heat or physical activity
  increases fluid loss.
- Water can be a primary source of hydration.
- Include water-rich foods in the diet.

Individual hydration needs vary.

Excessive water consumption is not beneficial and can be harmful.


---

## Crash Diets and Hair Health

Severe calorie restriction or rapid weight loss can affect
hair growth in some people.

### Better Approach

- Avoid extreme diets.
- Eat adequate calories.
- Include sufficient protein.
- Include a variety of nutrient-rich foods.
- Maintain gradual and sustainable dietary habits.


---

## Vegetarian and Vegan Diets

Plant-based diets can provide adequate nutrition when well
planned.

Useful sources can include:

- Lentils
- Beans
- Chickpeas
- Soy
- Nuts
- Seeds
- Whole grains
- Vegetables
- Fruits

Certain nutrients may require particular attention depending
on the individual's diet.

Professional dietary advice may be useful when nutritional
deficiency is suspected.


---

## Supplements

Supplements should not automatically be recommended for
hair growth.

Excessive intake of some nutrients can be harmful.

If nutritional deficiency is suspected, appropriate medical
evaluation and testing can help determine whether supplementation
is necessary.


---

## Overall Hair Nutrition Principle

There is no single food that guarantees healthy hair.

A sustainable approach includes:

- Adequate calories
- Adequate protein
- Fruits and vegetables
- Whole grains
- Legumes
- Healthy fats
- Adequate hydration
- Nutrient variety
- Avoiding extreme diets
- Addressing confirmed nutritional deficiencies appropriately
