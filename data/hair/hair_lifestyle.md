# Hair Care Lifestyle and Daily Habits

## Hair Washing

Hair-washing frequency depends on scalp oiliness, hair type,
activity level, climate, and personal preference.

### General Guidelines

- Wash the scalp regularly according to its needs.
- Avoid assuming that everyone should wash their hair at the
  same frequency.
- Cleanse after activities that cause substantial sweating
  when appropriate.
- Avoid excessively hot water.
- Massage the scalp gently rather than scratching it.


---

## Shampoo

Shampoo helps remove oil, sweat, dirt, and product buildup
from the scalp and hair.

### Good Practices

- Apply shampoo primarily to the scalp.
- Massage gently with fingertips.
- Avoid aggressive scratching.
- Rinse thoroughly.
- Choose a shampoo appropriate for your scalp and hair needs.


---

## Conditioner

Conditioner can help improve hair manageability and reduce
friction.

### Good Practices

- Apply conditioner mainly to the lengths and ends when
  appropriate.
- Avoid aggressive rubbing.
- Rinse according to product instructions.
- Adjust usage depending on hair texture and condition.


---

## Oiling

Hair oils can reduce friction and may improve the feel and
manageability of some types of hair.

### General Guidance

- Use a small amount if oiling is comfortable.
- Avoid leaving heavy oil buildup on the scalp.
- Stop if itching, irritation, or scalp problems develop.
- Oiling should not be considered a treatment for unexplained
  hair loss or scalp disease.


---

## Heat Styling

Frequent use of high-temperature styling tools can damage the
hair shaft.

Examples include:

- Hair dryers
- Straighteners
- Curling irons

### Prevention

- Reduce unnecessary heat styling.
- Use lower temperatures when possible.
- Avoid repeatedly applying high heat to the same section.
- Allow hair to dry naturally when practical.


---

## Tight Hairstyles

Repeated tension on the hair can contribute to breakage and,
in some cases, traction-related hair loss.

### Better Practices

- Avoid hairstyles that continuously pull on the scalp.
- Alternate hairstyles.
- Avoid tying hair extremely tightly.
- Pay attention to scalp pain or tenderness.


---

## Pillowcase and Hair Hygiene

Hair and scalp come into contact with pillowcases for several
hours each night.

### Helpful Habits

- Wash pillowcases regularly.
- Keep bedding clean.
- Choose a comfortable fabric that does not cause excessive
  friction.
- Avoid sleeping on visibly dirty bedding.


---

## Pollution and Environmental Exposure

Pollution, dust, heat, humidity, and other environmental factors
can affect scalp and hair comfort.

### General Habits

- Keep the scalp clean.
- Wash hair when necessary after substantial environmental
  exposure.
- Avoid excessive buildup of dirt and sweat.
- Protect hair from harsh environmental conditions.


---

## Wet Hair

Wet hair is more vulnerable to mechanical damage.

### Better Practices

- Handle wet hair gently.
- Avoid aggressive brushing immediately after washing.
- Use a soft towel or allow hair to air dry when practical.
- Avoid tightly tying wet hair.


---

## Combing and Brushing

Aggressive brushing can contribute to mechanical damage.

### Good Practices

- Detangle gently.
- Start from the ends and gradually work upward.
- Avoid pulling through severe knots.
- Use an appropriate comb or brush for the hair type.


---

## Hair Treatments

Frequent bleaching, relaxing, perming, coloring, or other
chemical treatments can weaken the hair shaft.

### Prevention

- Avoid unnecessary repeated chemical treatments.
- Allow adequate time between treatments.
- Follow professional or product safety instructions.
- Stop treatments if significant irritation occurs.


---

## Sleep and Stress

Adequate sleep and stress management support overall health.

### Helpful Habits

- Maintain a consistent sleep schedule.
- Aim for adequate sleep.
- Exercise regularly.
- Practice relaxation techniques.
- Take breaks during prolonged periods of work or study.


---

## General Hair Wellness

Healthy hair habits include:

- Appropriate washing
- Gentle handling
- Limited excessive heat
- Limited unnecessary chemical treatments
- Balanced nutrition
- Adequate hydration
- Adequate sleep
- Stress management
- Clean bedding and personal items
- Avoiding excessive tension on the hair
