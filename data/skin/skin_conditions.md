# Skin Conditions and Common Concerns

## Dry Skin

Dry skin occurs when the skin does not retain enough moisture. It may feel tight, rough, flaky, or uncomfortable.

Common lifestyle factors that can contribute to dryness include:

- Frequent exposure to dry air
- Hot showers or very hot water
- Excessive cleansing
- Low fluid intake
- Environmental exposure
- Lack of adequate dietary variety
- Stress and poor sleep

### General Self-Care

Helpful habits may include:

- Use lukewarm rather than very hot water.
- Avoid aggressive scrubbing.
- Pat the skin dry instead of rubbing.
- Keep the skin protected from harsh environmental conditions.
- Maintain adequate hydration according to individual needs.
- Eat a balanced diet containing vegetables, fruits, whole grains, legumes, nuts, seeds, and other nutritious foods.

---

## Oily Skin

Oily skin produces more sebum than some other skin types. Excess oil can contribute to a shiny appearance and may be associated with clogged pores.

### General Self-Care

Helpful habits include:

- Avoid repeatedly washing or scrubbing the face.
- Keep hands clean before touching the face.
- Avoid picking or squeezing spots.
- Keep pillowcases and towels clean.
- Maintain a balanced diet.
- Get adequate sleep and manage stress.

---

## Acne-Prone Skin

Acne-prone skin may develop pimples, blackheads, whiteheads, or other blemishes.

Lifestyle factors can sometimes influence skin health, although acne has multiple possible causes.

### General Self-Care

- Avoid squeezing or picking blemishes.
- Keep frequently touched items such as phones and pillowcases clean.
- Avoid harsh scrubbing.
- Maintain regular sleep.
- Manage stress where possible.
- Eat a varied and balanced diet.
- Seek professional advice when acne is severe, painful, persistent, or causing significant scarring.

---

## Sensitive or Easily Irritated Skin

Sensitive skin may react easily to environmental conditions or certain substances.

Possible signs include:

- Redness
- Burning
- Stinging
- Itching
- Dryness
- Discomfort

### General Self-Care

- Introduce changes gradually.
- Avoid harsh scrubbing.
- Avoid applying potentially irritating household substances to the face.
- Stop using something that clearly causes irritation.
- Seek professional advice if reactions are severe or persistent.

---

## Uneven Skin Tone

Uneven skin tone can have many possible causes, including environmental exposure and previous skin inflammation.

### General Self-Care

- Protect skin from excessive sun exposure.
- Avoid picking at blemishes.
- Maintain gentle skincare habits.
- Maintain a balanced diet.
- Get adequate sleep.
- Seek professional evaluation for new, rapidly changing, or concerning pigmentation.
