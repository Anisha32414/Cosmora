# Natural Skin Self-Care

Natural self-care should be simple, gentle, and low-risk.

## Oatmeal

Oatmeal can be used as a gentle skin-soothing ingredient.

A finely ground form of oatmeal can be combined with lukewarm water to create a simple soothing preparation.

Avoid vigorous rubbing.

---

## Aloe Vera

Aloe vera is commonly used as a soothing ingredient for skin.

If using aloe vera, use a clean preparation and stop if irritation occurs.

Avoid applying it to open wounds or seriously damaged skin.

---

## Gentle Moisture Support

Dry skin may benefit from practices that reduce moisture loss.

Helpful habits include:

- Avoid very hot water.
- Keep bathing or washing gentle.
- Pat the skin dry rather than rubbing.
- Apply appropriate moisture-supporting care after washing if tolerated.

---

## Cool Compress

A clean, cool compress may provide temporary comfort for minor skin irritation.

Do not apply extremely cold materials directly to the skin.

---

## Avoid Harmful DIY Ingredients

Cosmora should NOT recommend applying potentially irritating household substances directly to facial skin.

Examples include:

- Lemon juice
- Undiluted vinegar
- Baking soda
- Toothpaste
- Undiluted essential oils
- Strong alcohol-based substances

These substances can cause irritation or damage.

---

## Patch Testing

When trying a new topical natural ingredient, a small-area test can help identify possible irritation before broader use.

Stop using the substance if burning, significant redness, swelling, itching, or other concerning reactions occur.

---

## Do Not Overuse Natural Remedies

Natural does not automatically mean safe for everyone.

Avoid combining many new remedies at the same time.

Introduce changes gradually and observe how the skin responds.

---

## When to Seek Professional Help

Professional medical advice is appropriate when skin symptoms are:

- Severe
- Persistent
- Rapidly worsening
- Painful
- Bleeding
- Associated with swelling or pus
- Causing significant discomfort
- Unusual or concerning

Cosmora is an AI-based wellness assistant and does not provide medical diagnoses.
