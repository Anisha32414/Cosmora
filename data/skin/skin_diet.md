# Skin Diet, Nutrition, and Hydration

Nutrition supports overall health and provides nutrients needed for normal body functions, including maintenance of healthy skin.

No single food can guarantee prevention or treatment of a skin condition.

## Balanced Diet

A varied diet can include:

- Vegetables
- Fruits
- Whole grains
- Legumes
- Nuts
- Seeds
- Protein-rich foods
- Healthy sources of dietary fats

The goal should be dietary variety rather than extreme restrictions.

---

## Protein

Protein provides amino acids needed for normal tissue maintenance.

Useful dietary sources may include:

- Lentils
- Beans
- Chickpeas
- Peas
- Soy foods
- Eggs
- Dairy foods
- Fish or other animal protein when included in the individual's diet
- Nuts and seeds

---

## Fruits and Vegetables

Fruits and vegetables provide vitamins, minerals, fiber, and other beneficial nutrients.

Try to include a variety of colors and types throughout the week.

Examples include:

- Carrots
- Spinach
- Tomatoes
- Bell peppers
- Citrus fruits
- Berries
- Bananas
- Papaya
- Guava

---

## Healthy Fats

Healthy dietary fats can be obtained from foods such as:

- Nuts
- Seeds
- Avocado
- Olive oil
- Flaxseeds
- Chia seeds

---

## Hydration

Adequate hydration supports normal body functions.

There is no single universal amount of water that is appropriate for every person.

Individual fluid needs can vary based on factors such as:

- Body size
- Physical activity
- Climate
- Temperature
- Diet
- Sweating
- Individual health circumstances

Instead of following a rigid number, drink fluids regularly and respond to thirst and environmental conditions.

People with medical conditions affecting fluid intake should follow guidance from their healthcare professional.

---

## Limit Excessive Sugary Drinks

Frequently consuming large amounts of sugary drinks may make it harder to maintain a balanced diet.

Prefer water and other appropriate unsweetened beverages according to individual preferences and needs.

---

## Avoid Extreme Diets

Very restrictive diets may make it difficult to obtain adequate nutrients.

Cosmora should encourage balanced eating rather than crash diets, fasting for skin improvement, or unnecessary elimination of major food groups.

---

## Important Note

Food and hydration habits support general wellbeing but should not be presented as cures for skin diseases.

Cosmora provides AI-based wellness guidance and not medical diagnosis or treatment.
